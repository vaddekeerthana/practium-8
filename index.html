About Metaverse

Metaverse is a digital virtual environment that connects people through advanced technologies such as Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR) and Artificial Intelligence. It allows users to interact, communicate, explore virtual spaces, attend events and experience digital activities in an immersive three-dimensional world.
